# Yellowstone preview assets

Fictional collection artwork generated with the built-in image-generation tool. All six final PNGs are in `test/fixtures/assets/yellowstone/`. They are copied only to the isolated QA vault and are visibly marked SAMPLE.

Shared prompt: Create one standalone raster asset for a fictional stamp collection website. Pure white background, crisp deep cobalt blue rubber-stamp ink with subtle distressed texture. Flat scan, fully visible stamp centered with narrow white margins. Detailed engraved illustration matching a national park souvenir stamp, no photographic scene, no mockup objects, no other stamps. Small SAMPLE label is part of the design. Square canvas.

- `yellowstone-main.png`: Round blue ink souvenir stamp, central erupting Old Faithful geyser with pine trees. Text YELLOWSTONE NATIONAL PARK, smaller SAMPLE at bottom.
- `yellowstone-bison.png`: Rectangular blue ink souvenir stamp with a bison in profile, mountains and pine trees. Text YELLOWSTONE NATIONAL PARK, smaller SAMPLE.
- `old-faithful.png`: Round blue ink souvenir cancellation with an erupting geyser. Text OLD FAITHFUL and SAMPLE.
- `grand-prismatic.png`: Round blue ink souvenir cancellation with concentric spring rings and steam. Text GRAND PRISMATIC and SAMPLE.
- `yellowstone-canyon.png`: Round blue ink souvenir cancellation with a waterfall between canyon cliffs. Text YELLOWSTONE CANYON and SAMPLE.
- `mammoth.png`: Round blue ink souvenir cancellation with tiered travertine terraces. Text MAMMOTH HOT SPRINGS and SAMPLE.
